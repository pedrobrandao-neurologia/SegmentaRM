Template MNI152 2009c (ICBM 2009c Nonlinear Asymmetric, MNI152NLin2009cAsym), obtido do
TemplateFlow (https://www.templateflow.org), embutido de forma reduzida em lib/icv.js
(T1w 2 mm recortado ao intracrânio + 6 mm, 7 bits, e máscara intracraniana) para estimar
o volume intracraniano (eTIV) por registro afim.

Referências: Fonov VS, Evans AC, McKinstry RC, Almli CR, Collins DL. Unbiased nonlinear
average age-appropriate brain templates from birth to adulthood. NeuroImage 2009;47:S102.
Fonov V, Evans AC, Botteron K, Almli CR, McKinstry RC, Collins DL. Unbiased average
age-appropriate atlases for pediatric studies. NeuroImage 2011;54(1):313-327.
